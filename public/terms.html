Terms of Service

Effective date: November 2026

1. Marketplace Platform Relationship

GharKaam provides an online technological marketplace platform connecting independent domestic helpers (cooks, house cleaners, plumbers, electricians, caregivers) with customer households.

2. Code of Conduct & Dignity of Labor

All users of GharKaam—both customers and service partners—agree to interact with mutual courtesy, dignity, and respect. Discrimination, abusive language, or unsafe working environments will result in immediate and permanent account termination.

3. Bookings, Pricing & Invoices

Daily service rates are established collaboratively on the platform. The total booking fee comprises the helper's daily wages, a 5% platform facilitation fee, and statutory 18% GST. Customers may choose online payment or cash payment after service completion.

4. Cancellations

Bookings may be cancelled at zero cost prior to the commencement of service. Any payments collected will be promptly refunded to the customer's original payment method or wallet balance.
