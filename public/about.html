Our Mission

Bringing Dignity, Safety & Reliability to Every Home

GharKaam was established in 2026 to bridge the trust deficit between modern Indian households and the domestic helpers who keep those homes running.

For decades, finding domestic help relied on informal word-of-mouth, unverified backgrounds, fluctuating rates, and sudden absences. Meanwhile, hard-working cooks and domestic helpers lacked predictable income, safety nets, and respectful recognition of their vital labor.

GharKaam replaces friction with technology: government ID verification, predictable living wages, online bookings, transparent invoicing, and 24/7 safety oversight.

Uncompromising Safety

Every single helper must pass a multi-point government ID check (Aadhaar/Voter ID), permanent address verification, and continuous feedback auditing before joining our verified roster.

Dignity of Labor

We champion fair living wages, respectable treatment, prompt weekly digital payouts, and professional skill enhancement for domestic workers across India.

Complete Transparency

No hidden fees, no surprise broker cuts, and no opaque commissions. What you see is what you pay, backed by itemized GST invoices.

Our 4-Stage Verification Standard

1. Government ID Proof Validation

Original Aadhaar, Voter Card, or Driving License verified directly by administration.

2. Local Address & Residence Confirmation

Physical residence confirmation to ensure local accountability in each service district.

3. Prior Employer Reference Check

Structured reference inquiries confirming reliability, hygiene, and punctuality.

4. Continuous Quality & Rating Guard

Post-job customer reviews strictly monitored. Helpers falling below 4.0 are retrained or removed.
