GharKaam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal identification details such as your full name, phone number, email address, physical residence address, and pincode to enable accurate neighborhood matching and service dispatch. For helper partners, we also collect government identity proofs (Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN) strictly for background verification purposes.

2. Security of Verification Documents

Government ID proofs submitted by domestic helpers are stored in restricted access storage directories accessible exclusively by authorized background compliance personnel. ID document images are NEVER exposed on public profiles or shared with third parties.

3. Payment & Transaction Security

Online payment transactions are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant payment gateways (Razorpay). GharKaam does not store your credit card numbers, CVVs, or net-banking passwords on our servers.

4. Cookies & Local Storage

We use secure, httpOnly, sameSite cookies to preserve your authenticated user session. Client-side local storage is utilized strictly for non-sensitive preferences such as dark mode settings and language selection.

5. Contact Our Privacy Officer

If you have questions regarding your personal data or wish to request data deletion, contact us at privacy@gharkaam.com.
